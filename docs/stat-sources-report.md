# Stat sources: what is not safe

Generated by `node scripts/stat-sources-build.mjs` on 2026-11-10. Data in `gamedata/stat-sources.json`. Safe means two sources agree (the wiki and NEU, or the wiki and a value the app already has); everything below is one source or the sources differ. 38 values are safe, 73 are not.

## Not found

- Fairy soul exchange stats: there are none. The wiki says fairy souls stopped giving permanent stats in 0.15 (SkyBlock XP and backpack slots only). One source, not checked in game.
- Taming: no XP table on the wiki; the XP come from NEU alone, and NEU gives the cap as 50 where the wiki says 60.
- Runecrafting: no XP table or reward table on the wiki; XP from NEU alone, no stats in either.
- Enrichment values per rarity: neither source has any; the wiki gives one flat value and "Legendary or higher" as the requirement.
- Base stats of accessories: the item list has stats for 282 of 568 accessories and equipment pieces. The rest are only on the wiki, one page each, and are not collected. Of the 59 accessory and equipment ids the guides use, these have no stats: MAGIC_8_BALL, NETHER_ARTIFACT, RIFT_NECKLACE_INSIDE, RIFT_NECKLACE_OUTSIDE, WITHER_RELIC.
- SkyBlock level rewards besides +5 Health per level and +1 Strength per 5 levels: none listed.
- Social Wisdom in the Booster Cookie: the wiki says "on all Wisdom stats"; the list of skills is not given.

## The wiki disagrees with itself

- skills.social: XP Required: total at level 2 is 155, the sum is 150
